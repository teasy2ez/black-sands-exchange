🦴 Bones: How to Spend

Three ways to spend Bones — everything comes out of your current Spendable Bones balance.

🦴 Spendable Bones are deducted when used and never lower your Career Score on the Ledger.
Option 1

⭐ Rank Promotion

50 Bones

Climb the Black Sands hierarchy to unlock guild bank privileges and lower contract fees.

  • Perks Unlocked: Each rank unlocks expanded guild bank access and cheaper contracts.
  • Cost & Timing: Bones are spent immediately when you promote.
  • How to Promote: Upgrade directly from your Wallet HUD or use the button below.
Sign in with Discord to view your rank eligibility.
Option 2

🎲 Soft Reserves

20 Bones / SR

Put your Bones to work on extra Soft Reserve rolls in official guild raids.

  • SR Roll Cost: Spend 20 Bones per SR roll during guild raids.
  • Drop Protection: If your reserved item doesn't drop, 10 Bones are refunded.
  • Guild Raids Only: Applies exclusively to Black Sands guild raids — PUGs are never affected.
Active in Guild Raids Managed via Raid / Discord
Option 3

📜 Contracts (The Bounty Board)

Post on Website

Commission guildmates from the Bounty Board tab. There are two kinds of contracts:

🛡️ Help Contracts Helpers earn 5 Bones

Need a hand with a class quest, gold farm, alt leveling, or dungeon run? Every member who helps earns 5 Bones.

⚔️ Kill Contracts 1 Bone / Kill (Max 10)

Name a player or group you want dead. For 24 hours, any guild member who claims it earns 1 Bone per kill, up to 10 Bones each per contract.

Contract Cost by Rank (Applies to Both Kinds)
Rank 1 10 Bones
Rank 2 8 Bones
Rank 3 6 Bones
Rank 4 4 Bones
Rank 5 2 Bones
Rank 6 Free
🔒 Escrow Protection: Your Bones are held safely in escrow until an Officer approves your contract. If it's denied or cancelled, you receive a 100% full refund.
